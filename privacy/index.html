Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Image data

Photos selected in the background remover are read by your browser and processed locally. We do not upload them to our servers, store them in a database, or place them in object storage.

Static files and models

The site serves HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WebAssembly, and model files from Cloudflare's network. These files are public application assets, not user content.

Advertising

We may display Google AdSense advertising on content pages. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Those technologies do not receive the pixels of images you process in the tool.

Analytics

We prefer privacy-friendly, aggregate traffic measurement. If analytics are enabled, they should not receive image contents or personal files.

Cloudflare logs

As with any hosted website, Cloudflare may process ordinary request metadata such as IP address, user agent, and requested URL to deliver and protect the service.

Your choices

You can clear site data in your browser to remove cached application files. You can manage advertising cookies through your browser settings and Google's ad settings.
