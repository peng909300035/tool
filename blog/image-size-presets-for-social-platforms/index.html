Image size presets for social platforms and avatars

Common pixel dimensions for profiles, posts, and link previews, plus how to resize a photo locally to fit them.

Advertisement

Platforms change their recommended dimensions, but a few common sizes cover most needs. Resizing locally lets you prepare the right file without uploading an original to a third-party site.

Useful dimensions

  • 500 × 500 — a safe avatar size for most services.
  • 1024 × 1024 — square posts and catalog thumbnails.
  • 1080 × 1350 — portrait posts where more vertical space helps.
  • 1200 × 630 — link preview and social card images.
  • 1920 × 1080 — widescreen backgrounds and presentations.

Aspect ratio matters

Fill only the width or the height in a resizer and the other side follows the original ratio. That avoids stretching. Use exact dimensions only when the destination requires a specific crop.

Resizing in the browser

Our image resizer works with pixels, percentages, and presets. The file is processed through Canvas on your device, so the original photo is not uploaded.

Quality notes

Downscaling reduces file size but cannot add detail. Start from the highest-quality original you have, export at a sensible quality setting, and check the result at the size it will actually be displayed.
