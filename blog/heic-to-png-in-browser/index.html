Convert iPhone HEIC photos to PNG in your browser

Why HEIC files need decoding, and how a browser-based workflow avoids uploading private camera rolls.

Advertisement

iPhones often save photos in HEIC format. It stores good quality in a smaller file, but many websites and older tools expect JPEG or PNG instead.

What HEIC is

HEIC uses modern compression and can store depth information and sequences. Browser support varies, so conversion is often needed before editing or publishing.

Browser conversion

A local converter decodes the selected file, draws it on a canvas, and exports a PNG or JPEG. The original file stays on the device.

Quality notes

Converting HEIC to PNG preserves detail but increases file size. Use PNG when transparency or lossless output matters; use JPEG for ordinary photos.

Privacy benefit

Camera roll photos can contain personal details. Local conversion avoids sending an entire original file to a remote service just to change its format.
