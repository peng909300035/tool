How to resize a photo to 500×500 px for an avatar

A 500×500 square works on most profile pages. Here's how to crop and resize a photo locally instead of uploading it to a random site.

Advertisement

Profile picture fields are oddly inconsistent. Some want 400 pixels, some want 512, and almost all of them crop to a circle in the end. A 500×500 square is a safe choice that looks sharp everywhere without being a huge file.

Crop before you resize

This is the part people skip. If your photo is 4000×3000 and you shrink the whole thing to 500 pixels wide, the person ends up small in the middle with a lot of background around them. Crop to a square around the face first, then scale. Most of the time you want the eyes in the upper third of the frame.

Keep the ratio unless the site demands otherwise

Fill only the width in a resizer and the height follows the original proportion. That avoids stretching. Type both numbers only when the destination has a hard requirement, and expect to trim the edges a little.

Steps

  1. Open the image resizer.
  2. Drop in your photo and choose the 500×500 preset, or set the width and let the height follow.
  3. Check the preview. If the face is too small, crop first and try again.
  4. Download and use the square as your avatar.

Don't expect upscaling to help

If your original is 150×150, making it 500×500 just spreads the same pixels over a bigger area. Nothing sharper appears. You can smooth the result a little, but the detail isn't there to recover. Start from the largest original you have.

Check the file size too

Dimensions and file size are different things. A 500×500 PNG can still be a couple of megabytes if the photo is noisy. If the profile page has an upload limit, export as JPG at quality 80 and compare. Usually that brings it under 100 KB with no visible difference at avatar size.
