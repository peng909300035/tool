Create a transparent PNG for profile photos

How to export a clean cutout, choose the right canvas size, and avoid halos around hair and shoulders.

Advertisement

A transparent PNG is the safest format when you want to reuse a portrait on different backgrounds. Unlike JPEG, PNG can store an alpha channel, which tells the viewer which pixels should be invisible.

Start with a good source

Use a photo with even lighting and a background that contrasts with the subject. Avoid strong shadows behind the head, because dark shadows are often mistaken for hair.

Export settings

  • Choose transparent background, not solid color.
  • Use an evenly lit source photo for soft edges.
  • Keep the subject slightly away from the canvas edge.
  • Download the PNG and inspect it on both light and dark backgrounds.

Fixing halos

A pale outline usually comes from background pixels blended into the subject edge. It helps to use a source image with a plain background and to avoid enlarging the cutout far beyond the original resolution.

Reusing the file

Keep one master transparent PNG. Generate platform-specific versions from that master instead of removing the background again for every social network.
