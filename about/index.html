About

Small tools, fewer copies of your data

Toolorial builds focused browser utilities that do one job well and avoid unnecessary accounts or uploads.

Why we built it

Simple image tasks should not require sending private photos to a distant server. Modern browsers can run useful models locally, and static hosting makes that approach practical.

What we optimize for

  • Fast-loading pages with readable content
  • No account requirement for one-off tasks
  • Clear privacy language
  • Export formats that work in real workflows

How it is funded

Contextual advertising on written guides helps cover hosting and maintenance. The editing tools themselves do not send image data to those ad systems.
