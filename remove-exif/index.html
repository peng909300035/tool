FAQ

Remove EXIF questions

What is EXIF data?

EXIF is metadata stored inside image files. It can include camera model, timestamps, lens settings, and sometimes GPS coordinates.

Does this remove every metadata field?

The export is generated from decoded pixels, which strips the original metadata blocks. Open the result in a metadata viewer if you need to verify a specific field.

Does removing EXIF change the picture?

PNG output is lossless. If you choose JPG or WebP, the image is re-encoded at your chosen quality, so keep quality high for best results.

Why should I remove EXIF before sharing?

Location and device details can reveal a home address, workplace, or routine when several photos are published together.
