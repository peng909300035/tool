FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Toolorial free?

Yes. The browser-based tools are free to use. Advertising may be shown on content pages to support hosting and maintenance.

Are my photos stored anywhere?

No. Photos are read from your device into browser memory, processed locally, and exported back to your device. We do not write them to a database, object storage, or analytics system.

How does the background remover work?

The page downloads a small segmentation model to your browser. When you choose a photo, the model draws a subject mask locally and the page applies that mask with Canvas. Your photo is never uploaded to our server.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no upload queue. Choose an image and start processing immediately.
