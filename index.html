FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the background remover work?

The page downloads a small segmentation model to your browser. When you choose a photo, the model draws a subject mask locally and the page applies that mask with Canvas. Your photo is never uploaded to our server.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no upload queue. Choose an image and start processing immediately.

Does it work offline?

After the page, script, and model files have been cached by your browser, the core editing flow can continue without a network connection. Clearing site data removes that cache.

Which images work best?

Clear portraits, avatars, ID-style photos, and profile pictures work best. The current model is trained primarily for people, so complex product scenes or multiple distant objects may need manual cleanup.
