FAQ

Common questions

Is Toolorial free?

Yes. The browser-based tools are free to use. Advertising may be shown on content pages to support hosting and maintenance.

Are my photos stored anywhere?

No. Photos are read from your device into browser memory, processed locally, and exported back to your device. We do not write them to a database, object storage, or analytics system.

Why is a model downloaded on first use?

The segmentation model runs in your browser rather than on a server. It is downloaded once and can be cached for later visits.

Does advertising read my photos?

No. Ad scripts can set cookies or read standard browser signals for ad delivery, but they do not receive the pixels of images you process in this tool.

Can I use the result commercially?

You are responsible for the rights to the source image and for the final use of the exported file. Please review our Terms before publishing results.
